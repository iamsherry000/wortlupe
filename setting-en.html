WORTLUPE · iPhone SETUP

See German in WhatsApp, tap the back of your phone twice, look up every word

Wortlupe turns a whole German message into a reading page where every word is tappable: tap a word to see its base form, part of speech and English meaning, and open a grammar note for each sentence. Follow the three steps below once and you never have to set it up again.

  • 📱 iPhone
  • 🧭 Safari
  • ⏱ About 5 minutes
1

Add Wortlupe to your Home Screen

That way it works like an app, even without internet.

  1. Open this address in Safari (it has to be Safari; Chrome can't do this):
    https://wortlupe.iamsherry000.workers.dev
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add in the top-right corner.
  4. Go back to your Home Screen and open Wortlupe once. On that first launch it saves the dictionary to your phone, so it works offline from then on.
2

Create a “Wortlupe” shortcut

The shortcut sends the German you copy or share into Wortlupe. This is the longest step; just follow along.

  1. Open the built-in Shortcuts app. Can't find it? Swipe down on the Home Screen and search for “Shortcuts”.
  2. Tap ＋ in the top-right corner to create a new shortcut, and rename it Wortlupe.
  3. Open the shortcut's details:
    • Newer iOS: tap the shortcut name at the very top (it has a small ⌄ next to it) → Details.
    • Older iOS: tap ⓘ at the very bottom.
  4. In the details there's a switch called Show in Share Sheet. Just turn it on; that's all it takes for Wortlupe to appear in the Share menu.
  5. For the accepted input types, keep only Text and deselect everything else.
  6. Back in the editor, a new line Receive Text from Share Sheet now appears at the top. Tap If there's no input inside it and change it to Get Clipboard.
    This is the most important stepWith this change, the same shortcut works for both the Share menu and Back Tap.
  7. Tap the search bar at the bottom, find Combine Text and tap it to add it. Check that it says Shortcut Input, and set the separator to New Lines.
    This step makes forwarding several messages workWhen you share several WhatsApp messages at once, the shortcut receives several pieces of text. Without combining them first, they turn into several URLs and the shortcut fails.
  8. Then search for URL Encode and add it. Tap the variable inside it and change it to Combined Text.
  9. Then search for Text and add it. Paste this address into the text box:
    https://wortlupe.iamsherry000.workers.dev/#t=
    Leave the cursor at the very end and tap URL Encoded Text above the keyboard, so it's attached right after the address.
  10. Finally, search for Open URLs and add it. Check that it says Text.
  11. Tap Done in the top-right corner to save.
3

Set up “tap the back twice”

After copying German, you don't have to switch apps: just tap the back of your phone twice.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Accessibility → Touch → scroll all the way down to Back Tap.
  3. Tap Double Tap, scroll down to the Shortcuts section and choose Wortlupe.

Start using it

The first time, your iPhone asks whether the shortcut may read the clipboard and open a web address. Choose Allow for both.

  1. In WhatsApp, long-press a German message and tap Copy.
  2. Tap the back of your phone twice. Wortlupe opens with the text already in place.

Share menu

  1. WhatsApp: long-press the message → Forward → the Share icon in the bottom-right corner → Wortlupe.

FAQ

Wortlupe isn't in the Share menu

First check that Show in Share Sheet from step 2 is turned on. It may be far down the list: at the bottom of the Share menu, tap Edit Actions and move Wortlupe to the front.

Wortlupe opens, but the text isn't there

An old version may still be on your phone. Pull down on the Wortlupe page to refresh once. Also check that the address in step 2 ends with “URL Encoded Text”.

Forwarding several WhatsApp messages at once gives an error

The shortcut is missing the Combine Text step. Open the shortcut, add Combine Text (separator: New Lines) right below Receive, then change the variable in URL Encode to Combined Text. It should match the five blocks in the picture above.

Nothing happens when I tap the back

Make sure you chose Wortlupe in step 3. With a phone case, tap a bit harder, on the upper middle of the back.

Why does it open in Safari, not the Wortlupe on my Home Screen?

That's an iPhone limitation: shortcuts can only open web pages in Safari. Both work offline, and word lookup and grammar notes are exactly the same.

Are my messages sent over the internet?

No. The text goes after the # in the address, and browsers never send that part anywhere. Word lookup and grammar analysis happen entirely on your phone.
